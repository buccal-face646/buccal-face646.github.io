🤔 What Is This Kit?

Imagine you work at a company in Saudi Arabia or the UAE, and your team wants to use AI tools – maybe an AI assistant for customer service, or an AI system to review job applications. You might wonder: "Is this safe? Is it legal? Do we need permission?"

This free, open-source toolkit was built to answer those questions. It's a simple yet powerful assistant that helps you and your team handle AI responsibly, without needing to hire a lawyer or data scientist.

Everything runs directly in your web browser – no installation, no data uploaded, no technical skills required. It even works in Arabic and English, with full right-to-left support for Arabic text.

✨ What Makes This Kit Special?

📝 Register AI Use Cases

Keep a clear record of every AI system your organisation uses. Simple forms – no technical jargon – just answer a few questions about what the AI does.

🚦 Get an Explainable Risk Tier

The kit automatically assigns a risk level – like a traffic light (green, yellow, red). You'll know exactly why your AI got that score, so you can understand and act on it.

📤 Export Controls

Generate a professional PDF or spreadsheet report showing your AI inventory and risk assessments – ready to share with your managers, auditors, or regulators.

🌍 Bilingual AR/EN

Full Arabic and English support. Toggle between languages instantly. Right-to-left layout built-in. Perfect for GCC teams.

🔒 100% Browser-Based

No cloud, no servers, no account. Your data stays on your computer. The kit works offline – just open the HTML file in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).

📜 Aligned with Global Standards

Based on real frameworks like the EU AI Act, ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF, and designed with GCC specifics in mind – so you're on the safe side internationally.

💬 Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be a programmer to use this?

Absolutely not! Everything is visual – just click, type, and answer questions. The kit does all the complicated thinking for you.

Is my data safe?

Yes. The kit is 100% browser-based. Everything stays on your computer. Turn off the internet and it still works. No data goes to any server.

Can I use it on any Windows computer?

Yes. If you have Windows 10 or Windows 11 and any modern browser (Edge, Chrome, Firefox), you're all set. No extra software needed.

What if I need help?

Visit the GitHub page's discussion section or create an issue – the community is friendly and responsive, and the kit is free forever.
